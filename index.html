The Production Stack

One system. Six stages. Zero magic.

Every production AI system — including the one this course runs on — is a composition. Data boots up, gets retrieved, an agent loops over it with tools, state carries the memory, and evaluation catches what it misses. This is the machine you will learn to build.

The Curriculum

Ten modules. One shipped system.

Every module ends with your hands on the machine. Four of them have live labs on this page right now — the same labs, bigger, wait inside the course.

MODULE 00

How the Machine Thinks

Tokens, context windows, and why the machine sounds certain when it's guessing. The one mental model that explains everything AI does — why it forgets, why it invents, why order matters. Start here, and nothing downstream ever looks like magic again.

Open the Context Lab →
MODULE 01

The Boot Sequence

Before an agent thinks, it loads. Business facts, prices, rules, history, guardrails — stacked in the right order, every single time. Get the boot wrong and everything downstream is fiction.

Open the Boot Lab →
MODULE 02

Memory & State — Why AI Forgets

The machine forgets everything the moment a chat ends — unless you build it a memory. Working memory, long-term memory, and state: how a system holds a customer's name, last Tuesday's quote, and where a job stands — across days, not seconds.

MODULE 03

Retrieval, For Real

Not "upload a PDF and pray." Pre-retrieval, retrieval, post-retrieval — filtering, scoring, re-ranking — so your agent answers from the right page of your business, not a plausible one.

MODULE 04

The Agent Loop

Tools, tool tracking, state. The loop that turns a chat into a worker: answer, quote, book, follow up — in the right order, with a human safety net woven in.

Open the Playground →
MODULE 05

Voice, Vision & the Senses

Chat is only one sense. Give your system ears and eyes: phone calls it can hold, photos it can read, documents it can understand — the same senses running live today in Alice's phone receptionist and photo estimator.

MODULE 06

Fine-Tuning, Owner Edition

Train the system on your trade, your prices, your voice — no code. The owner-level version of fine-tuning: examples, corrections, and a knowledge bank that compounds.

MODULE 07

Evaluation — Catching What It Misses

Production systems are graded, not trusted. Score transcripts, hunt the planted misses — invented prices, dropped bookings, broken guardrails — and fix them before customers find them.

Play Miss Hunt →
MODULE 08

Guardrails, Privacy & Trust

Power without a fence is a liability. What the machine must never say, never store, never send — and when local beats cloud. The module that keeps your system, your customers, and your good name safe.

MODULE 09

Capstone — Ship It Live

No certificate for watching. You graduate when one production system — yours — is live, taking real requests from real customers, with you at the console. The capstone ships two things: the real system, and 🌐 your own developer portfolio site — the page that presents it to employers. Pass, and the 🏆 certificate of completion is awarded.

Module 00 · Lab

The Context Window

Everything the machine "knows" during a conversation lives in one window, measured in tokens — roughly one word each. This demo window holds 120. When it overflows, the oldest words fall out the back and stop existing. That's not the machine ignoring you; that's why it "forgot" your name. Fill it and watch.
